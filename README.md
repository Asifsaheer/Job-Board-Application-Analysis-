# Job board applications: three questions

Work sample on 1,206 made-up job board applications (July to September 2026).

## What is here
- `Job_Board_Applications_Work_Sample_FINAL.xlsx`: the whole analysis in one workbook.
  - **Note to Manager**: the three answers, one recommendation, what not to trust, and one chart.
  - **Sources & Categories**, **Funnel & Steps**, **Replies & Stipend**: the supporting calculations.
  - **Method & Notes**: cleaning, definitions, limits and tools used.
  - **Prepared Data**: the cleaned data, built with formulas from Raw Data.
  - **Raw Data**: the original CSV, untouched.

## How to re-run
Every number on the analysis tabs is a formula over Prepared Data, which links to Raw Data. Open the workbook and recalculate (Ctrl+Alt+F9) to reproduce the figures. Source data: https://www.insiderone.in/work-samples/data/job-board-applications.csv

## Cleaning
- 9 work-mode labels written with capital letters were standardised.
- 15 blank sources were set to Unknown.
- 6 exact duplicate rows were removed, leaving 1,200 applications.

## Tools used
Built and analyzed the project in Excel, including data cleaning, formulas, statistical calculations, and charts. I used AI tools mainly for guidance on Excel formulas, troubleshooting, and understanding statistical methods, without sharing the raw dataset. I independently performed the analysis, verified the results against the source data, interpreted the findings, and prepared the final recommendations.
